<h2><strong>About laser skin resurfacing treatment</strong></h2>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">If you want to reduce stubborn facial wrinkles, remove acne scars, or even out your skin tone and texture, laser skin resurfacing is one of the best options for achieving optimal results. </span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Laser resurfacing is also known as a laser peel or laser vaporisation because it uses concentrated beams of laser light energy to remove damaged skin, layer by layer, precisely.  </span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">The laser removes the outer layer of the skin (epidermis) and then heats the layers underneath to help promote the growth of new collagen. </span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">The results , you will see skin that looks smoother, firmer and radiant. </span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">We can use laser skin resurfacing treatment to target specific areas of your face, such as wrinkles around your eyes and mouth.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Alternatively, you can opt for treatment on your entire face for comprehensive skin rejuvenation. </span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">You can also have laser resurfacing treatment on other areas of the body, such as your hands, neck and décolletage. </span>
<h2><strong>Types of laser skin resurfacing: ablative vs non-ablative lasers</strong></h2>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">The two main types of laser resurfacing are: ablative lasers and non-ablative laser treatment. </span>
<h3><strong>Non-ablative lasers</strong></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Non-ablative treatments are more gentle than ablative lasers.  </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">This is because these devices do not remove the top layers of the skin. </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Instead, they work by heating the tissue beneath the skin's surface to promote collagen production. </span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">For this reason, non-ablative lasers, such as intense pulsed light (IPL), are better suited for treating mild skin tone issues, hair removal, and active acne. </span>

<em><strong>They do require multiple treatment sessions, more than an ablative laser. </strong></em>
<h3><strong>Ablative lasers</strong></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">This is a more powerful form of laser resurfacing, as it completely removes the top layer of skin. </span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">For this reason, ablative laser treatment is best suited for those with deep wrinkles, severe or moderate acne scars, and non-responsive skin (where other treatments have not been effective). </span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Erbium YAG laser resurfacing is a type of ablative laser treatment. </span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">It is one of the best fractional lasers because it delivers laser energy in a more controlled way. </span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">This means we can focus on treating skin conditions such as wrinkles and an uneven skin tone. </span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Fractional laser resurfacing, including Erbium YAG laser, is similar to carbon dioxide laser (Co2 laser). </span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">However, because it is more precise and causes less heat damage to surrounding tissue, it offers a much shorter recovery period.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;"> It also means that when set to the right parameters, Erbium YAG laser can treat darker skin tones.</span>
<h2><strong>How does laser skin resurfacing work?</strong></h2>
<h3><strong>Consultation</strong></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">The first step in laser resurfacing treatment is to have a consultation with one of our trained doctors. </span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">They will assess your skin concerns and review your medical history to determine if you are suitable for this treatment. </span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">You will need to have a patch test to ensure your skin type is suitable for treatment.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;"> This is especially true if you have very dark skin. </span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">If you have cold sores or active acne, our doctors will not recommend laser skin resurfacing treatment.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;"> In this instance, they will suggest alternative treatments that will suit your skin type and skin condition. </span>
<h3><strong>The procedure: laser resurfacing</strong></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Before you start laser skin resurfacing, your doctor will give you a local anaesthetic to numb the face. This will make the procedure more comfortable for you.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">If you are only having a small area of your face treated with laser treatment, you may be given a topical numbing cream to numb that area instead. </span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">You will be given an eye shield to protect your eyes from the laser light energy. </span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">During your procedure, the doctor will pass the laser beams over the areas of your face where you have skin issues. </span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">We will use specific laser points and depths to treat various skin concerns and types. </span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">After your erbium laser resurfacing session, your treated skin may appear red and slightly swollen. </span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">This is normal, and you should not be worried.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;"> Your doctor will apply a cream or ointment to the area to help soothe it and support the body's natural healing process.</span>
<h2><strong>Laser Skin Resurfacing: How Many Treatments Will I Need?</strong></h2>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">The number of facial resurfacing sessions you will need depends on the area being treated and your skin issues.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">For example, acne scars and deep wrinkles may require two to three treatments to achieve the best results. </span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">On average, our patients require 2 to 4 laser skin resurfacing treatments. </span>
