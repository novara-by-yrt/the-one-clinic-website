<h2><strong>What is Endolift treatment? Your alternative to a surgical facelift</strong></h2>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">If you are looking for a solution to loose skin, excess fat cells and moderate skin laxity, Endolift treatment could be the answer. </span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">It is a minimally invasive procedure that uses laser energy to directly target the most common signs of the natural ageing process. This includes sagging skin and lines and wrinkles. It helps by sculpting the face and bringing more health and vibrancy back to the skin. </span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">But the Endolift laser treatment does not stop there. It is also one of the latest fat reduction or body contouring treatments on the market. This is because the laser energy also targets excess fat cells, including facial fat and body fat to melt it away. </span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">It can do this because it works in the deeper layers of the skin to kick-start collagen production. This is a natural protein in our body that gives our skin its strength and structure. As we age, we lose levels of collagen in our skin, which is why skin starts to sag. Because Endolift increases collagen in the skin, it results in skin that looks more firm and radiant. </span>

<h2><strong>What to expect during Endolift treatment </strong></h2>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">You will have your Endolift treatment with one of our trained doctors in our Leicester clinic. During the treatment, we use a hand-held device that has a laser fibre. This is as thin as hair, which we insert under the skin. </span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Most patients describe feeling comfortable throughout the procedure. However, we do use a local anaesthetic to ensure your treatment is as comfortable as possible. </span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Once the laser fibre is underneath the skin, the laser-induced selective heat targets the fat and melts it. This also results in the skin retracting, which reduces sagging skin and loose skin. It is because of the high-powered nature of the laser that skin tightening occurs. </span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">The length of your treatment session will depend on which areas you are targeting. For one area of the face, it may only take ten minutes. Whereas the entire face can take up to one hour. </span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">To get the best results, you will need 1-2 Endolift treatment sessions. The exact number will be discussed during your consultation so that our doctor can tailor the treatment to your specific skin goals. </span>
