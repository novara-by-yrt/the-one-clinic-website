## Introduction

Endolift costs vary depending on treatment area, extent, and individual factors. At The One Clinic, we offer transparent pricing and flexible payment options to make this transformative treatment accessible.

## Typical Endolift Costs

### Full Face & Neck
**£1,800 - £2,500**
Comprehensive treatment addressing jowls, jawline definition, and neck sagging.

### Face Only
**£1,500 - £2,000**
Focused treatment on cheeks, jowls, and overall facial tightening.

### Neck & Jawline
**£1,200 - £1,600**
Specialized treatment for neck sagging and jawline definition.

### Smaller Areas
**£800 - £1,200**
Under-chin or isolated areas of concern.

## Factors Affecting Price

1. **Treatment Area Size** — Larger areas require more time and laser energy
2. **Severity of Sagging** — Extensive laxity may require longer treatment
3. **Consultant Expertise** — GMC-registered specialists may have different rates
4. **Facility Costs** — Purpose-built clinic with advanced technology
5. **Additional Services** — Combination treatments may affect overall cost

## Cost-Effectiveness Over Time

While Endolift has a higher upfront cost than dermal fillers, the long-term value is exceptional:

- **Endolift:** £2,000 treatment lasts 2-3 years = £55-80/month
- **Dermal Fillers:** £1,500/year × 3 years = £125/month
- **Facelift Surgery:** £6,000-12,000 with surgical risks
