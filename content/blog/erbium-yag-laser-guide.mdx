<h2><strong>What is laser skin resurfacing?</strong></h2>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">If you want to make a significant improvement to the tone and texture of your skin, then laser skin resurfacing treatment is worth considering.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Laser skin resurfacing is a cosmetic procedure that uses concentrated beams of laser light energy to remove the damaged outer layers of the skin. This allows new and healthy skin to grow in its place, which leaves you with a smoother and more radiant complexion. </span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">It is an effective laser treatment to help address and treat various skin disorders. This includes lines and wrinkles, sun-damaged skin and atrophic facial scars (from acne or infection).</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">There are different types of laser skin resurfacing treatments. At The One Clinic, we use the Erbium YAG laser. This is one of the most versatile and precise laser devices on the market. This means great results with less downtime and minimal thermal damage to the skin. </span>
<h2><strong>Introduction to Erbium lasers </strong></h2>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">The Erbium YAG laser (also known as Er:YAG laser) is a type of ablative laser, which removes the outer damaged skin layers. It does this by using infrared light to target and remove damaged skin while causing minimal thermal damage to the healthy surrounding skin. </span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">It is one of the most advanced lasers in the range of erbium lasers med sci for treating various skin issues through skin resurfacing. It is one of the most effective laser treatments for tackling and reducing deep wrinkles, acne scars, sun damage and age spots. </span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Erbium YAG laser uses power and precise laser energy to treat specific areas of the face such as around the eyes or mouth. Equally, it can treat the entire face or different parts of the body, including the hands, neck and décolletage.</span>

<h2><strong>How does Erbium YAG laser compare to CO2 lasers for skin resurfacing?</strong></h2>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Both the Erbium YAG laser and CO2 lasers are known as ablative laser treatments. This is because they remove the outer layers of the skin. </span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">One of the key differences is that CO2 lasers are more intense, which means that they can treat deeper acne scars and wrinkles. However, because they are more intense, they require a much longer downtime. They also run a higher risk of thermal damage and skin pigmentation issues, which is why they are not suitable for darker skin types. </span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">The Er:YAG laser can help you achieve similar results but without a lot of the side effects you get from CO2 laser treatment. This makes it safer for sensitive skin types and those with darker skin. </span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">The choice between the two laser skin resurfacing treatments will come down to your skin type, the skin issues you want treating and what results you want to achieve. </span>

<h2><strong>How the Erbium YAG laser works </strong></h2>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Your doctor will use the erbium YAG laser to emit a laser beam of light energy to target the damaged skin on the surface. This action vaporizes or removes the outer layers of the skin. </span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">The laser treatment works through stimulated emission, which produces a 2940 nm wavelength that is absorbed by the water in your skin. </span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Our doctors will use specific laser points and depths depending on your skin type and what skin concerns you are wanting to treat. </span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">At the end of your laser resurfacing session, the treatment area will look slightly red and swollen. It can also feel tight and sensitive. This is normal and should only last for a few days. We will provide you with aftercare advice that will help your skin heal and ensure you see great results. </span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">The results you see from laser skin resurfacing will differ from person to person. <span style="font-weight: 400;">Your skin will continue to get better over the next 2-3 months as the new collagen forms. This is when you should see a reduction in wrinkles, less pigmentation and smoother skin.</span>
