## Introduction

Erbium YAG laser technology represents one of the most advanced skin resurfacing treatments available today. Unlike CO₂ lasers, Erbium YAG offers precision with reduced thermal damage, making it ideal for patients seeking dramatic results with manageable downtime.

## How Erbium YAG Works

Erbium YAG lasers emit light at a wavelength (2940nm) that is highly absorbed by water in the skin. This precise energy:

- Vaporizes damaged outer skin layers
- Stimulates collagen production in deeper layers
- Tightens existing collagen
- Minimizes thermal injury to surrounding tissue

## Treatment Benefits

✓ Removes sun damage, age spots, and hyperpigmentation
✓ Smooths fine lines and moderate wrinkles
✓ Improves skin texture and tone
✓ Tightens loose skin
✓ Minimal downtime compared to CO₂ lasers
✓ Safe for darker skin types

## What to Expect

During treatment, you'll feel controlled heat as the laser resurfaces your skin. Post-treatment redness typically lasts 3-5 days, with full healing in 1-2 weeks.

## Recovery Timeline

- **Days 1-3:** Redness and minor swelling
- **Days 4-7:** Peeling begins, skin appears fresh
- **Weeks 2-4:** Continued improvement, results stabilize
- **Months 2-3:** Final results as collagen remodeling completes
