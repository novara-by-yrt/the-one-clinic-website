## Introduction

Endolift is a minimally invasive laser treatment with an excellent safety profile. Most side effects are mild and resolve within days to weeks. Understanding what to expect helps you prepare for a smooth recovery.

## Common Side Effects

### Swelling (48-72 hours)
Mild facial swelling is normal and typically peaks at 24-48 hours post-treatment.

### Redness (3-7 days)
Redness around treatment areas gradually fades within one week.

### Mild Discomfort
Some patients report mild tenderness or bruising, manageable with ice packs.

### Temporary Skin Texture Changes
Small areas may feel slightly rough as the healing process begins.

## Managing Side Effects

✓ Apply ice packs for 15 minutes, three times daily for first 48 hours
✓ Sleep with head elevated to reduce swelling
✓ Avoid heat, exercise, and intense sun for one week
✓ Use gentle skincare and prescribed aftercare products
✓ Stay hydrated

## Rare Side Effects

Serious complications are extremely rare with Endolift:
- Infection (less than 1%)
- Temporary numbness (resolves within weeks)
- Asymmetrical results (can be refined with touch-up)

## When to Contact Your Doctor

Seek immediate attention if you experience:
- Signs of infection (fever, pus, excessive warmth)
- Severe pain or swelling worsening after day 3
- Persistent numbness beyond 4 weeks
- Asymmetrical results after 2 weeks

## Final Thoughts

Endolift side effects are a normal part of the healing process. Most patients can return to normal activities within 3-5 days. Full results appear over 6-8 weeks as collagen remodels.
