<span style="font-weight: 400;">You may get rid of a sagging neck with simple home care, exercises, or professional treatments like EndoLift laser or radiofrequency procedures. </span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Saggy skin, sometimes called a “turkey neck,” happens when the skin loses its firmness, and the muscles weaken. This can happen from age, weight changes, or sun damage.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">In this guide, we explain what causes a saggy neck, easy things you can do at home, and the treatments doctors use to make your neck look firmer and smoother.</span>
<h2><b>What Is a Saggy Neck?</b></h2>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">A saggy neck occurs when the skin around the neck and under the chin loosens. The skin may hang or fold. Some people call this a “turkey neck”.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">On your neck, the skin is thinner and has fewer oil glands, which is why it dries out and weakens faster than the face.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Saggy neck cases may vary from mild, moderate, to severe. For some people, it is just soft skin. For others, there may also be extra fat or neck muscles.</span>
<h2><b>What Causes a Sagging Neck?</b></h2>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">A saggy neck does not happen for only one reason. A mix of changes in the skin and body usually causes it.</span>
<h3><strong>1. Ageing</strong></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">As we age, the skin makes less collagen. Collagen keeps skin firm. When collagen drops, the skin becomes loose and thin.</span>
<h3><strong>2. Sun Damage</strong></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Too much sun breaks down collagen faster. The neck is often missed when using sunscreen. Over time, this leads to sagging.</span>
<h3><strong>3. Weight Loss</strong></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">After weight loss, the skin may not shrink back fully, leaving loose skin under the chin and neck.</span>
<h3><strong>4. Genetics</strong></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Some people are more likely to get a saggy neck. This is passed down in families. Even with good care, sagging can still happen.</span>
<h3><strong>5. Lifestyle Habits</strong></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Smoking, poor diet, and low water intake harm the skin. These habits reduce blood flow and slow skin repair.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">When creams and home care do not give enough results, non-surgical treatments can help. These treatments are done by trained doctors. They tighten the skin from within and help the neck look smoother and firmer, without surgery.</span>

<h2><b>How To Remove Severe Saggy Neck Skin? Surgical Options</b></h2>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">When the skin is very loose or there is excess fat, surgery may give the best results. These procedures are done by experienced doctors and usually give long-lasting improvement.</span>
<h3><strong>1. Neck Lift/Facelift</strong></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">A neck lift removes extra skin and tightens muscles under the neck. It can also improve jawline shape and reduce sagging. Some fat may be removed at the same time. The results are dramatic and look natural when done carefully. Recovery usually takes a few weeks.</span>
<h3><strong>2. Neck Liposuction</strong></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Liposuction removes stubborn fat pockets under the chin and along the neck. It does not tighten loose skin on its own, so it is often combined with a neck lift. The procedure is done carefully to improve contours and give a smooth look.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">When non-surgical treatments are not helpful, or you want quick results, surgery might be the option.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">A consultation with a doctor is important to choose the safest option for your neck and goals.</span>
<h2><b>What are Natural Turkey Neck Remedies?</b></h2>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Natural care can help a saggy neck, especially in the early stages. These steps will not remove loose skin, but they can slow sagging and improve skin feel.</span>
<h3><strong>1. Skincare for the Neck</strong></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Use gentle creams made for the skin. Products with retinol, hyaluronic acid, or peptides help keep skin smooth and hydrated. Always apply skincare to the neck, not just the face.</span>
<h3><strong>2. Sun Protection</strong></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">The sun rays can be damaging foryour skin and sagging may appear very quickly. Use sunscreen on your neck every day, even in winter. This helps protect collagen and prevent further sagging.</span>
<h3><strong>3. Healthy Diet and Water</strong></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Drink water often during the day. Eat foods with vitamin C and protein. These help the skin stay strong and healthy.</span>
<h3><strong>4. Neck Exercises and Massage</strong></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Simple neck movements help muscle tone. Massage can help blood flow. These steps can improve the feel, but they cannot tighten loose skin.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Natural care works best for mild sagging. If the skin is very loose, medical treatments may be needed.</span>
<h2><b>What Exercises Can Reduce Neck Sagging?</b></h2>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Neck exercises can help tone the muscles under your skin and improve the appearance of a saggy neck. While they cannot remove loose skin, they can make your neck look firmer and more lifted.</span>
<h3><strong>1. Chin Lifts</strong></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Tilt your head back and look at the ceiling. Pucker your lips like you are kissing the ceiling and hold for 10 seconds. Repeat 10,15 times. This strengthens the muscles under your chin.</span>
<h3><strong>2. Tongue Presses</strong></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Tilt your head back and press your tongue to the roof of your mouth. Hold for 5,10 seconds and repeat 10 times. This works the neck and jaw muscles.</span>
<h3><strong>3. Neck Tilts and Stretches</strong></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Gently tilt your head from side to side or forward and backward. Hold each stretch for 5-10 seconds. These stretches improve blood flow and muscle tone.</span>
<h3><strong>4. Forehead Push</strong></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Place your palm on your forehead and push your head gently against it without moving your head. Hold for 10 seconds and repeat a few times. This strengthens front neck muscles.</span>
<h3><strong>5. Jaw &amp; Gum Exercises</strong></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Chewing gum or opening your mouth wide while holding a stretch can help tone jaw and neck muscles over time.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Tip: Do these exercises daily for the best results, but remember that significant loose skin may require professional treatment, such as EndoLift, laser, or radiofrequency, for a firmer look.</span>
