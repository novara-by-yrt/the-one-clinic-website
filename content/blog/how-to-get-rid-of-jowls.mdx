<span style="font-weight: 400;">The loose or saggy skin around your jawline is called jowls; they are a natural part of ageing. As we get older, our skin loses elasticity, fat pads shift, and bones slowly shrink, which can make the lower face look less defined.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">While jowls are completely normal, many people want to improve their appearance and regain a smoother jawline. Several treatments help address them, from non-surgical treatments and surgical procedures to simple lifestyle changes and natural methods. </span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Let this blog walk you through all the options so you can find what works best for you.</span>
<h2><b>Why Do Jowls Appear?</b></h2>
<p data-start="1041" data-end="1264">Jowls develop due to a combination of <strong>ageing, genetics, sun damage, lifestyle habits, and gradual loss of facial volume</strong>. These factors weaken skin structure and support around the jawline, causing the skin to sag over time.</p>
<p data-start="1266" data-end="1361"><em data-start="1269" data-end="1361">For a detailed explanation of causes and stages, read our <b><strong><a href="https://the-oneclinic.co.uk/blog/sagging-jowls-a-complete-guide/">complete guide on sagging jowls</a>.</strong></b></em></p>

<h2><b>How To Get Rid Of Jowls Without Surgery? </b></h2>
<img class="aligncenter wp-image-3440 size-full" src="/images/How-to-get-rid-of-jowls-infographic-showing-non-surgical-treatments-like-Endolift-laser-fillers-RF-thread-lifts-and-jawline-slimming.png" alt="How to get rid of jowls infographic showing non-surgical treatments like Endolift laser, fillers, RF, thread lifts, and jawline slimming" width="1080" height="1781" />
<h3><b>1. Endolift Laser</b></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Endolift uses a tiny laser fiber placed under the skin to gently tighten the jawline and stimulate collagen production. It can reduce sagging and contour the lower face, giving a lifted, smoother appearance without surgery. Results improve gradually over a few months as new collagen forms.</span>
<h3><b>2. Dermal Fillers</b></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;"><a href="https://the-oneclinic.co.uk/dermal-filler-leicester/"><strong><b>Hyaluronic acid fillers</b></strong></a> restore lost volume in the cheeks and jawline. By lifting the skin from underneath, they help smooth out sagging areas and improve facial contours. The effects are noticeable immediately and can last from several months to a couple of years.</span>
<h3><b>3. Ultrasound Therapy</b></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Ultherapy uses focused ultrasound energy to reach deep layers of the skin. It stimulates collagen production and gradually lifts and firms sagging jowls over a few months, with no downtime.</span>
<h3><b>4. Radiofrequency (RF) Treatments</b></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">RF treatments, such as Thermage or Morpheus8, use heat energy to tighten skin and encourage collagen production. For extra firming, in some cases, Morpheus8 is combined with RF with microneedling. These procedures gradually improve skin elasticity and contour the jawline.</span>
<h3><b>5. Thread Lifts</b></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">The professional inserts dissolvable threads under the skin to lift sagging jowls instantly. While lifting, they also trigger collagen production for ongoing improvement. Results are subtle but natural-looking and typically last about 12–18 months.</span>
<h3><b>6. Fat Reduction Treatments</b></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Techniques like CoolSculpting Mini or Endolift’s fat-reducing effect target excess fat under the jawline. Reducing this volume makes the jawline look tighter and more defined, especially in combination with skin-tightening treatments.</span>
<h3><b>7. Jawline Slimming</b></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Anti-ageing injections can relax certain muscles that pull the lower face downward. By softening these movements, the jawline appears smoother and lifted. Results usually appear within a few days and last several months.</span>
<h2><b>How To Tighten Jowls With Surgical Options</b></h2>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Surgery can provide the most noticeable and long-lasting results for sagging jowls, especially when non-surgical methods aren’t enough.</span>
<h3><b>1. Facelift (Rhytidectomy)</b></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">A facelift procedure is the most effective way to tighten the lower face by removing sagging skin. During the procedure, excess skin is lifted, and underlying muscles are tightened for a smoother jawline. Results are long-lasting and can dramatically restore a youthful facial contour.</span>
<h3><b>2. Neck Lift</b></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Often performed alongside a facelift, a neck lift targets sagging skin and fat around the neck and jawline. You can have a firmer, more defined lower face, reducing the appearance of jowls and turkey neck. Recovery takes time, but the results are significant and natural-looking.</span>
<h3><b>3. Mini Facelift</b></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">A mini facelift is less invasive than a full facelift and focuses on the lower face. It removes mild to moderate sagging and can lift jowls. This option is ideal for people in their 40s or 50s who want noticeable results without a long recovery period.</span>
<h3><b>4. Liposuction/Facial Liposculpture</b></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Liposuction removes excess fat deposits under the jawline and chin using small, precise incisions. By sculpting the area, it enhances the jawline and reduces sagging. This procedure is often combined with skin-tightening treatments for optimal results.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">For those looking for a dramatic and permanent improvement, surgical treatments remain the most reliable option to restore a firm, youthful jawline.</span>
<h2><b>What To Expect From Jowls Treatments?</b></h2>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">After any treatment for sagging jowls, knowing what to expect helps you plan and stay realistic.</span>
<h3><b>1. Immediate vs Gradual Results</b></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Some treatments, like fillers, provide instant improvement. Others, like lasers or RF treatments, work gradually as collagen builds over weeks or months. Surgical results are noticeable once swelling goes down, usually in 2-4 weeks.</span>
<h3><b>2. Recovery Timeline &amp; Side-Effects</b></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Non-surgical treatments usually have minimal downtime. You might see mild redness, swelling, or bruising that fades in a few days. Surgery has a longer recovery, including swelling and bruising, and normal activities resume in a few weeks.</span>
<h3><b>3. Longevity &amp; Maintenance</b></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Non-surgical treatments often need repeat sessions to maintain results. Fillers can last 6-24 months. Laser and RF results may last 1-2 years. Surgical results are longer-lasting, but ageing will continue naturally.</span>
<h3><b>4. Realistic Expectations</b></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">No treatment can stop ageing completely. The goal is improvement, not perfection. Combining healthy habits with professional treatments gives the best, natural-looking results.</span>
<h2><b>How Do You Get Rid Of Jowls Naturally?</b></h2>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Simple lifestyle habits can help slow sagging and keep your jawline firmer.</span>
<h3><b>1. Facial Exercises</b></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Facial exercises gently tone the muscles around your cheeks, jaw, and neck. Movements like chin tucks, neck stretches, or exaggerated mouth motions help lift and support sagging skin over time. Consistency is key, with exercises done a few minutes daily.</span>
<h3><b>2. Healthy Diet and Water Intake</b></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Eating a balanced diet rich in antioxidants, vitamins, and healthy fats nourishes your skin and promotes collagen production. Drinking enough water also keeps the skin plump and elastic, making jowls less noticeable.</span>
<h3><b>3. Good Posture and Sleep</b></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Keeping your head upright and sleeping on your back can prevent additional sagging. Poor posture and side-sleeping can allow gravity to pull on facial muscles and skin, worsening jowls.</span>
<h3><b>4. Sun Protection and Skincare</b></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Daily sunscreen use prevents UV damage, which breaks down collagen and elastin. Moisturisers and creams containing retinol or peptides can support skin elasticity and firmness.</span>
<h3><b>5. Avoid Smoking and Excess Alcohol</b></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Both smoking and excessive alcohol consumption speed up ageing, reduce collagen, and contribute to sagging skin. Reducing these habits can help slow down jowl development.</span>
<h3><b>6. Do a Facial Massage</b></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Gently massaging your face and neck can improve blood flow and stimulate collagen production. This can subtly lift the lower face and reduce tension in the jaw muscles.</span>
<h2><b>Do Facial Exercises Really Work For Jowls?</b></h2>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Facial exercises target the muscles in your jaw, cheeks, and neck. Some studies suggest they can help improve muscle tone and slightly lift the area.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">However, the effect on loose or sagging skin is limited. Exercises alone cannot tighten skin that has lost elasticity.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">A </span><a href="http://www.kptjournal.org/journal/view.html?doi=10.18857/jkpt.2021.33.6.297"><span style="font-weight: 400;">2021 review</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> found only limited evidence that facial exercises help with facial rejuvenation. They may be helpful as part of a broader plan, but professional treatments are usually needed for noticeable improvement.</span>
<h2><b>Do Jowls Go Away With Weight Loss?</b></h2>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Many people wonder if losing weight will make jowls disappear. While weight loss can reduce excess fat around the face, it does not always remove jowls completely.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Jowls are caused not only by fat but also by sagging skin, weakened muscles, and loss of elasticity. If the skin has already stretched, losing fat may make the sagging more noticeable rather than less.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">That said, maintaining a healthy weight can prevent additional fat from accumulating under the jawline and may improve the overall appearance of your face. Combining weight management with skin-tightening treatments or facial exercises can give better results than weight loss alone.</span>

 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Keep good posture and avoid straining your face.</span></li>
