<h3><strong>Your HydraFacial treatment – six steps to better skin health </strong></h3>
<h4><span style="font-weight: 400;">1.</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">   </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Detox</span></h4>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Step one is a lymphatic drainage massage, which helps draw out toxins from the skin. This process also increases circulation and oxygen levels in your skin. </span>
<h4><span style="font-weight: 400;">2.</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">   </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Cleansing and exfoliation</span></h4>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">In this step, we use a vacuum device to remove dead skin cells and extract impurities from the skin. This device works on both the surface and the deeper layers of the skin. </span>
<h4><span style="font-weight: 400;">3.</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">   </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Brightening</span></h4>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">To help clear clogged pores, we use a gentle solution that contains glycolic and salicylic acid. As this removes dead skin cells and other impurities, it also helps to brighten the skin. This step and step four help make HydraFacial a great acne treatment. </span>
<h4><span style="font-weight: 400;">4.</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">   </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Extraction</span></h4>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">The fourth step uses patented vortex fusion technology to remove blackheads and any remaining impurities from the skin. </span>
<h4><span style="font-weight: 400;">5.</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">   </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Hydration</span></h4>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">After a deep cleanse and extraction, we apply an antioxidant serum. This serum includes a mix of active ingredients, including hyaluronic acid, antioxidants, and peptides. This action gives your skin an instant boost of hydration and helps it look fresh and radiant.  </span>
<h4><span style="font-weight: 400;">6.</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">   </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Rejuvenation</span></h4>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">The final step uses LED light therapy to stimulate new collagen in the skin. This is much gentler on the skin than laser treatment and helps to calm redness. </span>
