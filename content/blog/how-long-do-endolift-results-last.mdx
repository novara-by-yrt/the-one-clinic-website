## Introduction

One of Endolift's greatest advantages is result longevity. Unlike dermal fillers (which last 6-18 months), Endolift results typically persist for 2-3 years because the treatment stimulates your body's own collagen production.

## Endolift Timeline: What to Expect

### Weeks 1-2
Initial results visible as swelling subsides and skin tightens.

### Weeks 4-8
Notable improvement as collagen remodeling accelerates. This is when most patients see dramatic transformation.

### Months 2-6
Continued subtle improvements as new collagen forms. Many patients report ongoing enhancement through 6 months.

### Months 6-24
Results plateau and stabilize. Your improved skin is your new baseline.

### 2-3 Years
Gradual softening as the stimulated collagen naturally breaks down over time.

## How Long Results Last

Most patients enjoy significant results for **2-3 years**. Some experience benefits for 3+ years depending on:

- Skin quality and genetics
- Age and natural collagen production
- Sun protection habits
- Overall skin health
- Lifestyle factors

## Maintaining Your Results

### Extend Results Naturally
✓ Consistent SPF 50+ application
✓ Healthy diet rich in antioxidants
✓ Adequate hydration
✓ Regular skincare routine
✓ Avoid excessive sun exposure
✓ No smoking

### Touch-Up Treatments
Many patients schedule a maintenance Endolift at 2-3 years to refresh results and sustain the improvement.

## Cost-Effectiveness Over Time

Because Endolift results last 2-3 years, the cost per month is significantly lower than dermal fillers requiring annual touch-ups.
