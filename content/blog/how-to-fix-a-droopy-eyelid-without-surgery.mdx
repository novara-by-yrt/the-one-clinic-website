<h2><b>Why Do Eyelids Droop?</b></h2>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Before you choose a treatment, you must know the cause.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Your eye is complex. It involves skin, muscle, fat, and bone. When an eyelid drops, it is usually because of one of two things.</span>
<h3><b>Loose Skin (Hooding)</b></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">This is the most common reason. As we get older, our skin loses collagen. Collagen is the protein that keeps skin tight. Without it, the skin on the eyelid stretches.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Gravity pulls this loose skin down. It creates a fold, or hood, that sits on top of your eyelashes. This is a skin texture issue.</span>
<h3><b>Heavy Brows (Gravity)</b></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Sometimes, the eyelid is fine. The problem is the forehead.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Your forehead skin also gets loose. When it slides down, it pushes your eyebrows down. This weight pulls the eyelid skin down. It looks like a droopy eyelid, but it is actually a droopy brow.</span>
<h3><b>Muscle Weakness (Ptosis)</b></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">This is different. This is when the actual muscle that opens the eye gets weak. The eyelid margin (the rim) sits lower on the eyeball. If your eyelid covers your pupil (the black part of the eye), you might have Ptosis. This is a medical issue, not just cosmetic.</span>
<h2><b>Why Creams Do Not Work?</b></h2>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">You see many adverts for eye lift creams.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Here is the truth: A cream cannot lift a heavy fold of skin.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Think of a pair of jeans that are too big. You cannot make them fit by rubbing fabric softener on them. You have to shrink the fabric or stitch it tighter.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">It is the same with eyelids. If you have excess skin, you need a physical treatment to shrink it or lift it.</span>
