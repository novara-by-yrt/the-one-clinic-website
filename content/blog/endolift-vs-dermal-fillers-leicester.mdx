## Introduction

When it comes to non-surgical facial rejuvenation, two treatments stand out: **Endolift** and **dermal fillers**. Both can address sagging skin, volume loss, and fine lines, but they work in fundamentally different ways.

If you're considering a lifting treatment in Leicester, understanding the differences between these two approaches will help you make an informed decision that aligns with your goals and budget.

## What Is Endolift?

Endolift is a **laser-based skin tightening treatment** that works from the inside out. A thin laser fibre is gently inserted beneath the skin's surface, where it:

- **Melts small fat deposits** for subtle contouring
- **Stimulates collagen production** through controlled heat
- **Contracts skin fibres** immediately, with continued improvement over months

**Best for:** Overall skin tightening, sagging skin, jowls, double chin, and gradual, natural-looking results.

## What Are Dermal Fillers?

Dermal fillers are **injectable gels** that instantly restore volume by filling lines and folds. Common fillers include:

- Hyaluronic acid fillers (e.g., Juvederm, Restylane)
- Biostimulating fillers (e.g., Profhilo, polynucleotides)

**Best for:** Targeted volume restoration, nasolabial folds, thin lips, and immediate results.

## Key Differences Explained

### Treatment Mechanism

**Endolift** addresses the root cause of sagging: loss of skin elasticity. By stimulating new collagen, it rebuilds your skin's structural support.

**Fillers** work on the surface, adding volume to smooth lines and restore fullness in specific areas.

### Timeline & Patience

With **Endolift**, patience pays off. Visible results appear within 4-8 weeks, with full results at 6 months. Many patients report continued subtle improvements for up to 9 months.

**Fillers** offer instant gratification—results are visible within hours of injection.

### Cost Considerations

A single Endolift treatment (£1,500-3,000) may seem expensive upfront, but the 2-3 year longevity makes it cost-effective long-term.

Fillers require regular touch-ups every 6-18 months, depending on the product. A full-face treatment with multiple syringes can be £1,200-2,000 annually.

## Which Treatment Is Right for You?

### Choose **Endolift** if you:

✓ Have noticeable skin sagging or laxity  
✓ Want a natural-looking, gradual transformation  
✓ Prefer fewer touch-ups long-term  
✓ Are willing to wait 6 months for optimal results  
✓ Want to address jowls, neck sagging, or double chin  

### Choose **Dermal Fillers** if you:

✓ Want immediate results  
✓ Have minimal downtime requirements  
✓ Prefer to target specific lines or lips  
✓ Want to test results before committing to more invasive options  
✓ Don't mind regular maintenance appointments

## FAQs

**Q: Can I do Endolift and fillers in the same session?**  
A: Yes, many patients combine both treatments on the same day or with a few weeks between appointments.

**Q: Will Endolift results last longer than fillers?**  
A: Yes. Endolift typically lasts 2-3 years, while fillers last 6-18 months depending on the product and area treated.

**Q: Is Endolift painful?**  
A: Endolift involves mild discomfort but is performed under local anaesthetic, so pain is minimal.

**Q: Can I combine Endolift with other treatments?**  
A: Absolutely. Many patients pair it with our Morpheus8 radiofrequency treatment or biostimulating fillers like Profhilo for enhanced results.
