## Introduction

Endolift aftercare is straightforward and requires minimal lifestyle disruption. Most patients can return to normal activities within 3-5 days. Following these guidelines ensures optimal healing and maximum results.

## Immediate Post-Treatment (First 24 Hours)

### Do's
✓ Apply ice packs: 15 minutes on, 15 minutes off for 6 hours
✓ Sleep with head elevated (use 2-3 pillows)
✓ Take prescribed pain relief if needed (usually minimal)
✓ Use gentle cleanser and lukewarm water
✓ Stay hydrated — drink 2-3 liters of water daily

### Don'ts
✗ Avoid hot water, steam, or saunas
✗ No intense exercise or heavy sweating
✗ Avoid alcohol and blood thinners
✗ Don't touch, rub, or scratch treated areas
✗ Skip makeup if possible
✗ No sun exposure

## Days 2-7: Active Healing

### Redness & Swelling Management
- Swelling typically peaks at 24-48 hours then subsides
- Redness fades progressively; use makeup if needed
- Continue ice packs if swelling persists
- Continue sleeping elevated

### Skincare Routine
- Gentle cleanser (lukewarm water, twice daily)
- Apply prescribed aftercare balm or moisturizer
- SPF 50+ sunscreen every 2 hours if any sun exposure
- Avoid active skincare ingredients (retinol, AHAs, BHAs)

### Activity Level
- Light walking is fine
- Avoid gym, running, or intense exercise
- Resume light desk work
- Avoid hot environments

## Weeks 2-4: Transition Phase

- Redness resolves completely for most patients
- Skin begins showing visible improvement
- Can resume normal exercise gradually
- Continue SPF 50+ daily
- Introduce gentle serums and moisturizers

## Months 2-6: Results Development

- Collagen remodeling accelerates results
- Continued skin tightening and improvement
- Results continue improving through 6 months
- Maintain sun protection
- Resume all normal skincare products

## Complete Aftercare Checklist

| Timeline | Action |
|----------|--------|
| **Day 1** | Ice, elevate head, gentle care |
| **Days 2-7** | Continue ice, use prescribed products, avoid heat |
| **Week 2** | Resume light exercise, introduce gentle skincare |
| **Week 3-4** | Full activity, standard skincare routine |
| **Months 2-6** | Enjoy developing results |

## Warning Signs — When to Contact Your Doctor

While complications are rare, contact us immediately if you experience:
- Severe pain or swelling worsening after day 3
- Signs of infection (fever, pus, excessive warmth)
- Persistent numbness beyond 4 weeks
- Severe bruising or unusual symptoms

## Maximizing Your Results

✓ Avoid smoking — it impairs collagen formation
✓ Eat protein-rich foods — supports tissue repair
✓ Stay hydrated — promotes healing
✓ Protect from sun — preserves new collagen
✓ Avoid excessive heat — don't stress healing tissue
✓ Be patient — best results appear over 6-8 weeks
