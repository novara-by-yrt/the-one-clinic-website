<h2><span style="font-weight: 400;">What Is CO2 Laser Resurfacing?</span></h2>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">CO₂ laser treatment uses a carbon dioxide laser to remove thin layers of skin. The laser targets water in skin cells, causing old, damaged cells to break down and shed. At the same time, the laser's heat stimulates collagen production deep within the skin.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Collagen supports your skin, keeping it firm and smooth. As your skin heals after </span>laser CO2 skin resurfacing<b>,</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> it produces more collagen, which helps tighten the skin and improve texture over time. </span>CO2 laser facial<span style="font-weight: 400;"> treatments are designed to maximise these benefits while targeting specific areas such as fine lines or scars.</span>
<h2><span style="font-weight: 400;">Two main types of </span>CO2 laser Treatment<span style="font-weight: 400;">:</span></h2>
<ul>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Ablative CO2 laser</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">: removes the outer skin layer completely for dramatic results</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Fractional CO2 laser</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">: targets tiny columns of skin, leaving surrounding tissue untouched for a faster recovery</span></li>
</ul>

<h2><span style="font-weight: 400;">How Does </span><b>CO2 Laser Skin Resurfacing</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Work?</span></h2>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">CO₂ laser resurfacing uses focused laser energy to gently remove damaged outer layers of skin and stimulate new skin growth.</span>

<b>Simple Explanation:</b>
<ul>

 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">It also stimulates collagen production in deeper layers of the skin.</span></li>

</ul>

<h2><span style="font-weight: 400;">The Downsides And Clinical Risks</span></h2>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">While highly effective, patients must carefully consider the potential downsides of a co2 laser face treatment before proceeding. Because the procedure is highly intensive, it requires significant clinical commitment.</span>

<b>Significant Downtime: </b><span style="font-weight: 400;">Unlike lighter, non-ablative treatments, this intensive procedure typically requires one to two weeks of dedicated recovery time as the damaged skin completely sheds and heals.</span>

<b>Temporary Side Effects:</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Patients typically experience severe redness, a noticeable feeling of warmth, and moderate swelling immediately following the session, similar to a very deep sunburn.</span>

<b>Risk of Hyperpigmentation:</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> There is a higher risk of post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation in darker skin tones. A medical professional must carefully assess the skin to ensure the treatment is safe for the individual.</span>

<b>​​Careful aftercare is important:</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Patients should follow a daily routine of strong sun protection and regular moisturizing to avoid infection and scarring while the skin heals.”</span>

<h2>FAQs About CO2 Laser Resurfacing</h2>
<h3>1. How painful is CO2 laser resurfacing?<span style="font-weight: 400;"> </span></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">A topical anaesthetic cream is applied before the treatment to reduce pain and keep you comfortable. During the procedure, you may feel a warm or prickling sensation. Most people find it very manageable.</span>
<h3>2. How long do CO2 laser results last?<span style="font-weight: 400;"> </span></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Results from CO2 laser skin resurfacing can last several years. Taking care of your skin regularly and protecting it from the sun will help the results last longer.</span>
<h3>3. Can a CO2 laser be used on all skin types?<span style="font-weight: 400;"> </span></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">CO2 laser treatment is most commonly recommended for fair to medium skin tones. Those with darker skin tones may need a fractional approach or an alternative treatment to reduce the risk of pigmentation changes.</span>
<h3>4. Can I wear makeup after CO2 laser treatment?<span style="font-weight: 400;"> </span></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">You should avoid wearing makeup during the initial healing phase, which is typically the first 5 to 10 days. Your practitioner will advise when it is safe to resume normal skincare and makeup use.</span>
<h3>5. Is one CO2 laser session enough?<span style="font-weight: 400;"> </span></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">For many skin concerns, one session delivers excellent results. However, some people may benefit from a second session depending on the depth of the concern and the results achieved after the first treatment.</span>
