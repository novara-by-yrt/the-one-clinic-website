<h2><span style="font-weight: 400;">What is Verruca Exactly and How is it Caused?</span></h2>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Verruca or plantar warts are an infection that traces back to HPV (human papillomavirus), which is a group of viruses entering the skin through cuts or abrasions. When inside, the virus then projects outwards on the top layer of the skin, creating a verruca. It appears like a small rough patch of skin and can hurt your sole when pressed. </span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">However, not everyone exposed to HPV will develop verrucas. There are a number of factors that can cause the human papillomavirus to result in a wart. The </span>verruca healing stages <span style="font-weight: 400;">will depend on it. These are a few causes of verruca growth:</span>
<ul>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Skin-to-skin contact with someone who has warts.</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Walking barefoot in moist and warm areas like changing rooms, swimming pools, and gym showers.</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Minor skin injury that leaves small scrapes on your feet</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">A weak immune system makes it difficult for the body to fight HPV.</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Indirect contact by using the infected person's towel, razors or other personal items.</span></li>
</ul>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">As a verruca needs a cut or a crack to enter your skin, children are more susceptible to the condition due to their constant activities. Moreover, the children’s immune system is weaker and is still building defences against unencountered viruses.</span>
<h2><span style="font-weight: 400;">How to Identify a Verruca?</span></h2>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Yes, the question is how to spot a verruca in the first place. Or are you confusing it with calluses? Before understanding </span>verruca healing stages, <span style="font-weight: 400;">knowing what it actually looks like is important. Here's how they show up:</span>
<ul>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Texture:</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> It is a rough lesion on the skin of your sole that commonly appears on the ball or heel of the foot and the toes.</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Black dots: </b><span style="font-weight: 400;">The plantar wart will always have tiny black dots in the centre, which are actually clotted blood vessels.</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Abnormal skin lines:</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> If you notice a disruption in the normal lines of your skin, it is a clear sign to differentiate a verruca from a corn. Warts break the natural ridges of the skin.</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Pain: </b><span style="font-weight: 400;">When the verruca is under pressure, i.e., when the person stands, they may feel pain from the infected area.</span></li>
</ul>
<h2><span style="font-weight: 400;">The Key Signs that Show Verruca is Healing</span></h2>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Whether you have undergone professional treatment by a specialist or performed a home remedy, there are a few changes that will indicate you</span>r verruca healing stages.

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Your verruca may be a single lesion, small in size or in a large cluster around your heels. <h3><span style="font-weight: 400;">Change in Texture: </span></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">The wart seeds, which are dotted with black blood clots in the middle, become more pronounced and dark, which indicates the tissue is dying. Plus, the overall colour turning to black or brown is a good sign.</span>
<h3><span style="font-weight: 400;">Peeling and Flaking: </span></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">As the verruca heals, the skin around it dries out, cracks, and even peels. The infected skin becomes crusty. This especially happens when you treat it with salicylic acid or freezing treatments.</span>
