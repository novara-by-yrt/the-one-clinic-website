<h2><strong>How dermal fillers and anti-wrinkle injections can help your skin look ten years younger </strong></h2>
<h3><strong>Dermal fillers vs anti-wrinkle treatment</strong></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">There is the common misconception that anti-wrinkle treatment and dermal filler treatment do the exact same thing. While they are both treatments that target the visible signs of ageing, what they do and the results they achieve are very different. </span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Anti-wrinkle treatment, also known as Botox, uses botulinum toxin type A, which is a protein that blocks the signals between the nerves and facial muscles. This causes the muscles to stop contracting for a few months. The result – Botox treatment helps to smooth away the fine lines and wrinkles that form when you smile, laugh or frown. These wrinkles are known as dynamic wrinkles. </span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Dermal fillers, on the other hand, are an injectable treatment that uses a gel-like substance to add volume and plump the skin. Dermal filler treatment can help to restore lost volume while also adding definition and shape to different areas of the face such as the cheeks, chin and jawline. </span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Another benefit of dermal fillers is that they boost hyaluronic acid and collagen in the skin. These are two natural substances in the body that we lose with age but that are vital to keeping the skin strong, flexible and looking healthy.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">While anti-wrinkle treatment targets dynamic wrinkles, dermal fillers target the static wrinkles that remain on the face even after you stop using your muscles. Fillers help plump and fill these deep lines and wrinkles to give you a smooth and even appearance. </span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">It is possible to combine these treatments, but you may have to have them separately to allow for both to do their job and give you the best results. </span>

<h3><strong>Which lasts longer – dermal fillers or Botox?</strong></h3>
<h4>Botox results</h4>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">It takes up to two weeks for the anti-wrinkle injections to relax the facial muscles. This is when you will see your final results and fewer lines and wrinkles. </span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Your results should last for 3-4 months. For some people, this can be longer. At this point, your muscles will return to their normal function, and the lines and wrinkles will start to reappear. </span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">At the three-month mark, we recommend that you book a top-up appointment to ensure you </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">maintain your results. </span>
<h4>Dermal filler results</h4>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Straight after your dermal filler treatment, you will see some visible improvements in the plumpness and stricture of your skin. This will continue to improve over the next two weeks as the fillers settle into place. This is when you will see your final results. </span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Dermal fillers last longer than Botox. Most patients see the results of their dermal fillers for 9-12 months. This will depend on how your body responds to the fillers, your lifestyle and how quickly your body breaks them down.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">We recommend that you come back to the clinic at the 9-12 month mark to have a top-up treatment. This will ensure your skin stays looking smooth, plump and rejuvenated. </span>
