<h2><b>What Are Malar Bags?</b></h2>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Malar bags are the soft, puffy areas that sit just under your lower eyelids. They often sit on top of the cheekbone and can make the under-eye area look swollen or tired.</span>
<h3><b>How They Look and Feel:</b></h3>
<ul>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Soft, slightly swollen puff under the eyes.</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">May feel rubbery when pressed.</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Can make the eyes look tired or heavy.</span></li>
</ul>
<h3><b>How Malar Bags Differ From Other Under-Eye Concerns:</b></h3>
<ul>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Under-eye bags</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">: Usually caused by fat or fluid moving forward; more noticeable when tired.</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Festoons</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">: They are larger, sagging folds that hang over the cheek; often linked to fluid retention.</span></li>
</ul>
<h2><b>Why Do Malar Bags Develop?</b></h2>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Malar bags form for a combination of natural and genetic reasons. Here are some common causes of malar bags:</span>
<ul>
 	<li aria-level="1"><b>Skin Loosening - </b>As we get older, our skin gets looser. Loose skin can make bags under the eyes more visible.</li>
</ul>
<ul>
 	<li aria-level="1"><b>Fat Pad Bulging- </b>Fat under the eyes can move forward. This creates puffiness and small bulges.</li>
</ul>
<ul>
 	<li aria-level="1"><b>Fluid Buildup- </b>Sometimes fluid collects under the eyes, making the area look swollen.</li>
</ul>
<ul>
 	<li aria-level="1"><b>Genetics- </b>Some people naturally have a weaker under-eye structure. Malar bags can run in families.</li>
</ul>
<h2><b>What Is Endolift?</b></h2>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Endolift is a laser treatment done under the skin. It helps tighten skin and reduce puffiness, and is often used for malar bags under the eyes.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Endolift uses a very thin laser fibre that is gently placed under the skin to tighten it and stimulate collagen and elastin production. There are no cuts or stitches, and the entry point of the injury heals on its own.</span>
<h2><b>How Does Endolift Work for Malar Bags?</b></h2>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Endolift works from inside the skin. It targets the real causes of malar bags, not just the surface. </span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Here’s how Endolift for malar bags works:</span>
<h3><b>1. Reduces Fat</b></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Some malar bags are caused by small fat pockets. Endolift uses gentle laser energy to melt this fat. The body then clears it away naturally over time. This helps reduce puffiness and fullness under the eyes.</span>
<h3><b>2. Tightens Undereye Skin</b></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Loose skin can make malar bags look worse. EndoLift laser heats the deep skin layers, causing the skin to tighten from within. The area looks smoother and more lifted.</span>
<h3><b>3. Increase Collagen Production</b></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">As we age, collagen levels drop. Endolift wakes up collagen production again. New collagen forms over the next few months. This makes the skin firmer over time.</span>
<h3><b>4. Drains the Fluid</b></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Malar bags often hold trapped fluid. EndoLift treatment helps improve drainage in the area. This reduces long-term swelling and heaviness, so that the area looks fresher and less puffy.</span>
