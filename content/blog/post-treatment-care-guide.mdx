## Introduction

You've booked your aesthetic treatment and completed the appointment. Now comes the critical phase: **aftercare**.

The first 24-72 hours following your treatment are crucial. What you do—and don't do—during this window directly impacts your results, downtime duration, and risk of complications.

This guide covers aftercare for the most popular treatments at The One Clinic.

## Universal Aftercare Rules (All Treatments)

Regardless of which treatment you've had, follow these core principles:

### 1. **Avoid Heat for 48 Hours**
- No hot showers or baths
- No saunas, steam rooms, or hot tubs
- No intense exercise or sweat-inducing activities
- No direct sunlight

**Why:** Heat dilates blood vessels and increases inflammation, swelling, and bruising.

### 2. **Keep Your Hands Off Your Face**
- Don't touch, rub, or scratch the treated area
- Avoid makeup for at least 24 hours (some treatments 48 hours)
- Don't apply other skincare products except those recommended by your doctor

**Why:** Your skin barrier is compromised post-treatment. Contact introduces bacteria and can cause infection or disrupted healing.

### 3. **Stay Hydrated**
- Drink 2-3 litres of water daily
- Hydrated skin heals faster and shows better results

### 4. **Sleep with Your Head Elevated**
- Use 2-3 pillows to reduce fluid pooling
- This minimises swelling, especially after injectables or laser treatments

### 5. **Use SPF 50+ Daily**
- Sun exposure can cause permanent pigmentation and damage new skin
- Apply sunscreen every 2 hours if outdoors

## Treatment-Specific Aftercare

### **Laser Resurfacing** (Lumecca, Laser Genesis, CO₂)

**First 24 hours:**
- Expect redness, mild swelling, and possible oozing
- Apply ice packs (10 minutes on, 10 minutes off) for the first 6 hours
- Use prescribed healing ointment every 2-3 hours
- Keep skin clean and dry

**Days 2-7:**
- Redness peaks around day 2-3
- Mild peeling and flaking begin
- Continue ointment application; don't pick at peeling skin
- Use gentle cleanser (no scrubbing)
- Apply broad-spectrum SPF 50+ daily

**Weeks 2-4:**
- Peeling subsides; skin looks fresher
- Continue sun protection religiously
- Introduce gentle moisturiser and basic skincare

**Timeline:** Redness fades by week 3; full results at 2-3 months.

### **Morpheus8** (Radiofrequency Micro-needling)

**First 24 hours:**
- Expect redness, mild warmth, and minimal swelling
- Ice packs reduce inflammation
- Avoid makeup and active products

**Days 2-5:**
- Redness fades significantly
- Skin may feel slightly sensitive
- Continue SPF 50+
- Can resume light exercise by day 3

**Weeks 2-8:**
- Collagen remodelling occurs beneath the surface
- Results gradually improve
- Avoid intense sun exposure

**Timeline:** Visible results by week 4; optimal results at 8 weeks. Results continue improving for up to 3 months.

### **Dermal Fillers & Injectables** (Profhilo, Juvederm, Restylane)

**First 24 hours:**
- Expect mild swelling, redness, and possible bruising
- Sleep elevated to reduce swelling
- Apply ice for 10 minutes per hour (first 6 hours only)
- Avoid makeup if possible

**Days 2-7:**
- Swelling peaks around day 2-3, then subsides
- Bruising may worsen before improving (use makeup to cover)
- Avoid massage or pressure on the treated area
- No intense exercise or hot activities

**Week 2+:**
- Swelling resolves
- Results stabilise
- Bruising fades (vitamin K cream can speed this)

**Timeline:** Initial results visible within 24 hours; full results at 2 weeks.

### **Chemical Peels** (TCA, Salicylic Acid)

**First 24-48 hours:**
- Expect visible peeling or crusting (depends on peel strength)
- Use prescribed post-peel balm or moisturiser
- Avoid water contact initially (follow your doctor's specific timeline)
- No makeup

**Days 3-7:**
- Heavy peeling; don't pick or force peeling
- Use gentle cleanser, warm (not hot) water
- Moisturise frequently
- SPF 50+ every 2 hours

**Weeks 2-4:**
- Peeling subsides; new skin is fresh and glowing
- Continue SPF diligently
- Introduce gentle serums or light treatments only

**Timeline:** Redness resolves by week 2; results improve for up to 3 months.

### **HydraFacial**

**Immediately & First 24 Hours:**
- No downtime; can resume normal activities
- Skin may feel slightly warm or flushed
- Avoid makeup for 2-4 hours
- Use gentle cleanser that evening

**First Week:**
- Skin is more receptive to serums and treatments
- Hydration peaks; skin looks plump and glowing
- Continue with your regular skincare routine
- Use SPF during the day

**Timeline:** Results visible immediately; benefits last 5-7 days. Recommend monthly treatments for maintenance.

## What to Avoid After Treatment

| Activity | Why | Duration |
|----------|-----|----------|
| Hot baths/showers | Increases inflammation | 48 hours |
| Exercise/sweating | Risk of infection; increases swelling | 48-72 hours |
| Makeup | Can introduce bacteria | 24-48 hours |
| Skincare actives (retinol, AHA, BHA) | Over-irritates treated skin | 1 week |
| Alcohol | Thins blood; increases bruising | 24-48 hours |
| Intense sun exposure | Risk of pigmentation damage | 2 weeks |
| Facials or other treatments | Can compound irritation | 2 weeks |
| Touching/rubbing the area | Risk of infection or scarring | 1 week |

---

## Signs Something Is Wrong

Most side effects are normal and temporary. However, **contact your doctor immediately if you experience:**

⚠️ Severe pain or swelling that worsens after day 3  
⚠️ Signs of infection (pus, excessive warmth, fever)  
⚠️ Severe or spreading rash  
⚠️ Blistering or severe burns (laser treatments)  
⚠️ Vision changes or eye swelling (eye area treatments)  
⚠️ Asymmetrical results 1 week after injectables

## Maximise Your Results: The 4-Week Protocol

**Week 1:** Healing phase—focus on protection and gentle care  
**Week 2:** Enhancement phase—introduce supporting skincare (serums, moisturiser)  
**Week 3:** Maintenance phase—consistent SPF, gentle routine  
**Week 4:** Results evaluation—book follow-up if needed  

---

## When Can You Resume Normal Activities?

- **Light exercise:** Day 2-3 post-treatment
- **Intense exercise:** Day 5-7 (depending on treatment)
- **Makeup:** 24-48 hours
- **Skincare actives:** 1 week
- **Sun exposure:** Avoid for 2 weeks; then SPF 50+
- **Next aesthetic treatment:** 2 weeks minimum
